# Research Notes

BIT Capital's general strategy is not to hold companies for a long time with significant positions. They have high turnover of investments.

They invest only in publically listed companies.

They've made a large push towards memory and storage over 2026, moving from 10% share of book for these investments at the end of 2025 to ~29% by mid-2026. This was driven by a huge spike in the value of Micron (800%+) which was sold down with the funds redeployed to other memory and storage companies such as Sandisk and Silicon Motion.

They have a "Devil's Advocate" system where any shares holding baove NAV 5% have to be intensely justified. One can see this clearly with positions on shares that have grown enormously being sold down, e.g. Micron.

They have held a few shares for a long time, like IREN, Amazon. 

I would say they are betting on coming spike to data centers for AI, given the increase in holdings in memory companies. 

Consider where investments have investments in frontier AI labs - there's a direct link here.

Given the turnover of stocks - the very active nature of the investments - they're looking for buy trends as well as sell trends. Sometimes they sell and rebuy into a position later.
