You are deciding whether two articles from the same AI lab describe the **same
event** and are **redundant** with each other.

Answer `same` only if BOTH of these hold:

1. They describe the same underlying event or announcement — not merely the
   same subject, the same product, or the same kind of event.
2. One is redundant given the other: a reader who has seen one learns nothing
   materially new from the other.

If either fails, answer `different`.

## What does not make two articles the same

- **The same subject.** A model launch and a safety disclosure about that model
  are two events. If one reports a threshold, a benchmark, a price or a risk
  that the other does not, they are different however similar they read.
- **The same kind of event.** Two partnership announcements naming two
  different partners are two partnerships.
- **The same template.** Customer case studies share almost every sentence and
  differ in the customer, the numbers and the outcome. Those differences are
  the content.

## What does make them the same

- One is a restatement of the other on a second channel — a blog post and its
  forum announcement, a page and its syndicated copy.
- One is a shorter summary of the other carrying no fact the longer lacks.

## The two errors are not equal

Merging two different articles **removes a claim from the product** — the
folded one stops being read, and nobody finds out. Failing to merge two copies
leaves a visible duplicate row, which is untidy and self-correcting.

So when you are genuinely torn, answer `different`, and say in the reason what
made it close.

## Worked examples

| A | B | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| "GPT-6 Astra: A new generation of intelligence" (launch blog) | "Introducing GPT-6-Astra: The most intelligent and aligned model in the world" (forum restatement) | `same` — one launch, second adds nothing |
| "GPT-6 Astra: A new generation of intelligence" | "Safety overview: GPT-6 Astra" | `different` — the overview reports the model reached the Critical cybersecurity level under the Preparedness Framework, a claim the launch does not carry |
| "TCS and Anthropic bring Claude to regulated industries" | "DXC integrates Claude into systems" | `different` — same kind of event, two companies, two deals |
| "Legora reviewed 41 documents in minutes with GPT-6 Astra" | "Playco cut manual fixes 50% prototyping games with GPT-6 Astra" | `different` — two customers, different facts |

## Output

Return JSON only:

- `same` — boolean.
- `reason` — one sentence, at most 25 words, naming the fact that decided it.
  If `different`, name the specific claim one carries and the other does not.
